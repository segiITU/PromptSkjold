# PromptSkjold: brand and design notes

Short record of the rebrand and the UI rewrite (2026-11-01 to 2026-11-02). Open items are in `../../TODO.md`.

## Name
**PromptSkjold** (same in Danish and English). It replaces "Privacy Guardrail". DFKI's name, logo and branding are not
licensed for reuse (`NOTICE`, `FORK_NOTES.md`); the Apache-2.0 attribution to DFKI stays.

## Logo
- **Mark:** a hand-drawn marker shield with a thick black redaction bar across it and two teal text lines above and two
  below the bar (text being blacked out, like a page). Teal tile with rounded corners for the app icon.
- **How it was chosen:** the user liked a hand-drawn marker look (shield + bar) and rejected a flat/geometric redraw, the
  olive branch and "variants of the same logo". Tried and dropped: tick mark, risograph offset, hatching, a redacted
  person, a monogram P, palm, marker pen, tilted stamp. A die-cut paper "sticker halo" is used for transparent versions on
  dark surfaces.
- **Icon sizes** (`src/assets/icons`): 16 and 32 px are the plain shield and bar (no text lines, they blur); 48 and 128 px
  include the lines.
- **Source art** is kept outside the repository (rejected variants, the cut-out hand source and the lockup files). `design/logo/` holds the exported logo files.

## Wordmark and lockups
- **Wordmark:** "PromptSkjold" hand-lettered in black marker with a thick underline bar that breaks where the p and j
  descend ("strike" variant), optional teal dot. Generated with the image-generation repo (google/gemini-3.1-flash-image,
  about $0.067 per image), then cut out to transparent PNG (light and light-on-dark versions).
- **Lockups:** shield right of the wordmark (no dot, transparent; a halo version for dark surfaces), plus compact,
  stacked and horizontal variants. In the extension the options page and the onboarding guide use
  `src/assets/lockup.png` (shield to the right, 52 px / 40 px high). The popup header still uses plain text.
- Final files are being edited by hand by the user; see TODO.

## Colour
Paper `#f3f2ef`, desk `#e7e5e0`, sheet `#fbfaf7`, ink `#1b1b1a`, teal `#3ec6bd`, text-safe teal `#1b625d`. Category
colours (all at least 4.5:1 on paper) and the rest live in one source, `src/shared/design/tokens.json`, which generates
the page CSS (`tokens.vars.css`) and the Shadow DOM block (`design-tokens.generated.ts`); a test fails if they drift.
Never edit the generated files; run `node scripts/build-tokens.js`.

## Type
IBM Plex Sans (UI, titles, wordmark fallback), IBM Plex Serif (review letter text only), JetBrains Mono (placeholders and
code). All SIL OFL 1.1, bundled as Latin subsets with their licence files in `src/assets/fonts` and `dist/fonts`; the
in-page bundle embeds three of them as base64 (`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`). Earlier ermias.ai fonts (Public Sans, Boska,
Anak Bocil) were dropped because the user disliked the look.

## Icons
Entity icons come from one source, `src/shared/design/icons.json`, generated by `scripts/build-icon-set.js` (edit the
generator, not the JSON). Current set: 1.5 px marker-style strokes with a category-colour blob and a highlighter swipe.
`entity-icons/` holds the sheet, a 7x pixel check and the SVGs; `entity-icons/variants/` holds the thin set. One icon per
finding card in the overlay, none inside the highlighted text.

## Language
Danish default, English alternative, one dictionary under `src/shared/i18n/` (key parity and placeholders enforced by a
test). The language follows the manual choice in the popup/options ("Automatisk" follows the browser). Danish term for
blacking out is "sorte felter" / "sort over" (never "sorter ud", which means "sort out").

## Design decisions worth keeping
- Draft A ("Papir": light, paper and ink, review as a letter on a sheet) was chosen over a dark editorial draft and a
  marker draft. The three drafts are in `../../../ui-drafts/` (outside this repo).
- The first version of the popup had too much going on; it was returned to the original information architecture and
  re-skinned. The overlay was decluttered: one highlight per finding, one icon, confidence only when low.
- Test tab: detected entities are highlighted in their category colour (not colour alone: title and hidden category text).
- Excel/PowerPoint checking is off by default; with it off, those files pass through with a "Slå til" notice.
