# Support

PromptSkjold is a public beta. Support is focused on Chrome desktop stable and the supported beta sites listed below.

## Public Support

Use GitHub Issues for:

- bugs
- supported-site compatibility reports
- false positives and false negatives
- documentation problems
- feature requests
- general questions

Use synthetic or sanitized examples only. Do not paste real personal data, secrets, private prompts, private responses, or private documents into public issues.

## Sensitive Reports

Send sensitive security or privacy reports to `kontakt@ermias.ai`.

## Public Beta Support Scope

Supported browser:

- Chrome desktop stable

Supported sites:

- ChatGPT at `chatgpt.com`
- ChatGPT at `chat.openai.com`
- Claude at `claude.ai`
- Gemini at `gemini.google.com`
- Microsoft Copilot at `copilot.microsoft.com` and `copilot.com` (selectors not yet verified on the live site)

Chrome and Edge (Chromium) are the only supported browsers. Sites you add yourself in the options page use a generic adapter and are not tested by the project; other browsers and sites are outside the public beta support scope.

## Before Filing

Check whether:

- the extension is installed and enabled
- the supported site tab was refreshed after installation or update
- the issue reproduces with synthetic text
- Local AI status is visible and matches what you expected
- pattern-based detection still runs when Local AI is unavailable or off

PromptSkjold provides assistive local review. It does not guarantee perfect detection, prevention, or regulatory compliance.
